<div class="page">

    <header class="page-header">
      <div class="eyebrow">Research</div>
      <h1>Computational science for molecular understanding and design</h1>
      <p>
        We work across computational chemistry, biomolecular modelling,
        cheminformatics and AI/ML, with applications in healthcare,
        sustainability and circular economy.
      </p>
    </header>

    <div class="cards">
      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">AI / ML</span>
        <h3>AI-driven molecular discovery</h3>
        <p>
          Chemical and biological representation learning, property prediction,
          molecular prioritization, interpretable modelling and generative design.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">Proteins</span>
        <h3>Computational protein engineering</h3>
        <p>
          Sequence, structure and dynamics of proteins, with emphasis on
          selective binding, functional pockets and protein–ligand interactions.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">Simulation</span>
        <h3>Molecular modelling &amp; simulation</h3>
        <p>
          Molecular dynamics, enhanced sampling, structural analysis and
          computational approaches for understanding molecular mechanisms.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">Healthcare</span>
        <h3>AI for healthcare</h3>
        <p>
          Computational approaches for molecular recognition, drug discovery,
          biomolecular interactions and health-related molecular systems.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">Sustainability</span>
        <h3>Critical-mineral recovery</h3>
        <p>
          Molecular design and AI for selective recognition, separation and
          recovery of critical elements from complex matrices.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="card">
        <span class="label">Circular economy</span>
        <h3>Sustainable molecular design</h3>
        <p>
          Computational strategies for designing molecules and materials that
          support resource recovery, sustainable chemistry and circular systems.
        </p>
      </article>
    </div>

    <div class="section" style="padding-left:0;padding-right:0;">
      <div class="prose">
        <h2>Our computational workflow</h2>
        <p>
          A typical project moves through a connected sequence:
          <strong>data curation → molecular representation → prediction →
          mechanistic analysis → design → validation → refinement</strong>.
          The exact workflow is adapted to the scientific question and the
          available experimental evidence.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

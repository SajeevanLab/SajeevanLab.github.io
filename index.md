# Sajeevan Lab

### Computational & AI-Driven Molecular Science

**Srinivasa Ramanujan Institute for Basic Sciences (SRIBS), Kerala, India**

We develop computational and AI-driven approaches to understand, design, and engineer molecular systems for challenges in healthcare, sustainability, and the circular economy.

## Research

- AI-driven molecular discovery
- Computational chemistry and molecular modelling
- Protein engineering and molecular design
- Sustainable drug discovery
- Critical mineral recovery
- AI for healthcare and healthy ageing

## Current Research

Our research integrates molecular simulations, cheminformatics, machine learning, protein language models, and data-driven molecular design.

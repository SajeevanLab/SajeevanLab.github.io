<section class="academic-section">
  <div class="container narrow">

    <div class="section-kicker">Research</div>

    <h2>
      From molecular understanding<br>
      to molecular design
    </h2>

    <p class="lead-text">
      Our research lies at the interface of computational chemistry,
      molecular simulation, protein and biomolecular science,
      cheminformatics and artificial intelligence.
    </p>

    <p>
      We are particularly interested in developing computational approaches
      that connect molecular structure, physical principles and reliable
      scientific data with predictive and generative models. These approaches
      are applied to problems spanning molecular discovery, biomolecular
      engineering and sustainable molecular science.
    </p>

  </div>
</section>

    <div class="eyebrow">Research</div>
    <h2>From molecular understanding to design</h2>
    <p class="section-intro">
      Our work sits at the interface of computational chemistry, molecular
      simulation, biological modelling, cheminformatics and machine learning.
      We aim to connect reliable molecular data and physics-informed descriptors
      with predictive and generative models.
    </p>

    <div class="cards">
      <article class="card">

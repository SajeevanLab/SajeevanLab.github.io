      <section id="catrange">
        <h2>CatRange</h2>
        <p>
          <strong>CatRange enables robust prediction of enzyme variant kinetic regimes.</strong>
          The project develops an enzyme-kinetics prediction framework that
          incorporates catalytic awareness and biological guardrails to improve
          prediction of variant kinetic behaviour from sequence and reaction
          information.
        </p>
        <p>
          <a href="https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgag309" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PNAS Nexus · DOI</a>
        </p>
      </section>

      <section id="anion-binding">
        <h2>Anion-binding proteins</h2>
        <p>
          We investigate computational strategies for identifying and designing
          protein pockets with selective recognition of anions. The work combines
          structural analysis, protein-language-model representations and
          machine learning, with an emphasis on understanding binding preference
          and guiding future experimental design.
        </p>
      </section>

      <section id="cyclome">
        <h2>Cyclome</h2>
        <p>
          Cyclome is a large-scale computational study of cyclic peptides,
          using molecular dynamics and enhanced-sampling approaches to examine
          conformational stability and molecular interactions across a diverse
          structural collection.
        </p>
      </section>

      <section id="q-select">
        <h2>Q-Select</h2>
        <p>
          <strong>AI-Driven Quantum Chemistry and Coordination Chemistry-Informed
          Design of Selective Chelators for Sustainable Critical Mineral Capture.</strong>
          This SRIBS seed-grant project integrates chemistry-aware data curation,
          quantum-chemical descriptors, orbital-informed graph representations,
          predictive machine learning and generative molecular design to
          prioritize selective chelators for critical-mineral capture.
        </p>
        <p>
          The proposed workflow includes computational prioritization followed
          by experimental validation in complex matrices and iterative
          comparison between prediction and experiment.
        </p>
      </section>

      <div class="eyebrow">About the lab</div>
      <h1>Scientific questions first. Computation as a bridge to design.</h1>
      <p>
        Sajeevan Lab is a computational molecular-science research group at the
        Srinivasa Ramanujan Institute for Basic Sciences (SRIBS), Kerala.
      </p>
    </header>

    <div class="prose">
      <p>
        The lab develops computational and AI-driven approaches for molecular
        discovery and engineering. Our work combines molecular simulation,
        computational chemistry, cheminformatics, machine learning and
        structure-based analysis to address problems where molecular-level
        understanding can enable better design decisions.
      </p>

      <p>
        A central principle of the lab is to connect <strong>data, physics,
        chemistry and biological function</strong>. Rather than treating machine
        learning as an isolated prediction step, we are interested in workflows
        that incorporate chemical validity, mechanistic information,
        interpretable descriptors and experimental feedback wherever possible.
      </p>

      <h2>Research philosophy</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Build reliable and chemically meaningful datasets.</li>
        <li>Use molecular simulation and quantum-chemical information to understand mechanisms.</li>
        <li>Develop predictive models that are tested for generalization and interpretability.</li>
        <li>Use generative approaches for molecular design where appropriate.</li>
        <li>Close the loop through experimental validation and model refinement.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Institutional home</h2>
      <p>
        SRIBS is a constituent of the Kerala State Council for Science, Technology
        and Environment (KSCSTE) and works across the fundamental sciences.
        The institute's research ecosystem includes chemistry, biology, physics,
        mathematics and computer science.
      </p>
